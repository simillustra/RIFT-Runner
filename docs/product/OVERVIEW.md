# Product Overview

## Vision
RIFT RUNNERS is a mobile-first third-person 3D action-survival infinite runner combining lane traversal with free-movement combat. Players survive escalating threats in fictional environments inspired by real-world places.

## Core experience
- Third-person traversal with lane-aware movement and contextual free movement.
- Responsive melee and ranged combat, combos, dodging, and character abilities.
- Procedurally assembled modular environments and escalating encounters.
- Three planned heroes: Vanguard, Phantom, and Cipher.
- Offline-first single-player as the foundation; online cooperative play is a later capability.

## Vertical-slice target
The first playable slice is planned to include the three heroes, core movement and combat, a boss encounter, modular environments (Infected City and Alien Facility), five enemy archetypes, guided virtual joystick and free-hand gesture input, basic progression/rewards, HUD/settings, Android test-build plus iOS project/build validation.

## Delivery priorities
- P0: playable offline vertical slice and production foundation.
- P1: Nakama-backed accounts, co-op (2- and 4-player architecture), and AI intelligence platform.
- P2: AI-assisted content generation and broader live operations.

## RR-001 non-goals
No hero gameplay, combat, procedural level generation, enemy behavior, online accounts, multiplayer, monetization, or AI runtime integration is implemented in the foundation task.
