# Product Requirements Document
**Product:** RIFT RUNNERS | **Status:** Baseline, subject to playtesting.

## Vision
Mobile-first third-person action-survival runner combining lane-aware traversal and free-movement combat in fictional, real-world-inspired environments.

## Core requirements
Offline single-player; three heroes (Vanguard, Phantom, Cipher); hybrid traversal; melee/ranged combat, combos, dodge and signature abilities; Infected City and Alien Facility; five planned enemy archetypes and one boss; joystick and free-hand gesture controls; HUD/settings/basic progression; Android test build and iOS project/export validation.

## Priorities
P0 offline vertical slice and quality foundation. P1 Nakama identity/co-op and AI intelligence platform. P2 AI-assisted content generation.

## Measures
Track tutorial completion, session length, run completion, combat readability, crash-free sessions, frame time, memory and retention. Numeric targets await device baselines.

## Exclusions
Live co-op, real-money purchases, remote AI enemy control and full live-ops are outside the first slice.
