# RR-007 D5.3 — Runtime encounter enemy spawning

## Objective

Connect the deterministic EncounterSpawnDirector plan to live enemy instances while preserving segment streaming ownership, authored archetypes, bounded concurrency, and encounter-local squad coordination.

## Runtime flow

1. ProceduralWorldGenerator assigns a monotonically increasing segment_index as segments are generated and emits segment_spawned.
2. A child EncounterRuntimeSpawner validates its configuration and listens to segment_spawned, segment_recycled, and generation_reset.
3. The spawner asks EncounterSpawnDirector.build_spawn_plan() for the segment's deterministic budget, transforms, seed, and concurrency cap.
4. It selects authored EnemySpawnDefinition resources with deterministic weighted selection, instantiates their PackedScenes, applies EnemyArchetypeProfile, and positions enemies at the planned transforms.
5. Each segment receives one EnemySquadCoordinator; spawned EnemyAIController instances are assigned that shared coordinator.
6. On segment recycle, targets and attacks are cleared, collision shapes and Areas are disabled, and enemies are detached into a bounded pool keyed by spawn-definition ID. A later matching spawn restores their authored collision/process state. Overflow is freed.
7. generation_reset clears active encounter bookkeeping and disposes pooled enemies.

## Contracts and ownership

- The director remains a plan producer; it does not instantiate nodes.
- The generator owns segment lifetime; the runtime spawner owns enemy lifetime only while its segment is active or pooled.
- Encounter concurrency is capped by the plan's max_concurrent and available authored spawn points.
- Enemy definitions require a valid Node3D scene, a valid archetype profile, and a unique stable ID.
- Runtime AI, movement, combat, and damage remain deterministic local Godot systems. No remote AI calls are introduced.
- The spawner is optional. Existing generator scenes without it retain current behavior.

## Automated validation

tests/encounter_runtime_spawner_test.gd verifies configuration, plan-to-instance spawning, encounter-scoped coordinator assignment, archetype application, segment release, bounded pooling, and pool reuse. CI runs this test after importing the project and registering global script classes.

## Acceptance boundary

This milestone wires the existing attack-slot coordinator into spawned encounters. It does not claim that RR-008 retreat/disengagement policy, tactical repositioning, or full role-based group behavior are complete; those remain D5.2 work. It also does not prove Android/iOS performance or gameplay fairness. End-to-end streaming/combat performance baselines remain D5.4.
