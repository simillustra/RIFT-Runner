# RR-008 D5.2 — Stagger, Retreat and Group Coordination

**Status:** Submitted for CI validation · Godot 4.4.1 · Typed GDScript

## Runtime contract

- Stagger interrupts attack execution, releases attack capacity, suppresses normal decisions for the reaction duration, then resumes target evaluation. Death remains terminal; a zero-duration stagger recovers on the next decision tick.
- Archetypes may configure a low-health retreat threshold (zero disables it) and retreat distance. Retreat cancels attacks, publishes squad status, steers away from the target and returns to ALERT after disengagement.
- A 10 percentage-point health recovery hysteresis prevents repeated retreat threshold chatter.
- Archetypes request ASSAULT, FLANKER or SUPPORT roles. The encounter coordinator reserves bounded flanker/support roles per target; duplicate requests deterministically fall back to ASSAULT. FLANKER members use deterministic lateral repositioning with per-instance side selection; SUPPORT members hold a wider standoff distance.
- Role reservations and retreat status are cleared on target change/loss and death.

## Ownership

The coordinator arbitrates roles and publishes group state; it does not own AI state, choose targets, move actors or apply damage. EnemyAIController owns transitions, EnemyChaseController owns steering, HealthComponent owns health and RR-005 owns combat/damage. All decisions remain deterministic and local.

## Acceptance coverage

The D5.2 test covers role capacity/fallback, low-health retreat, retreat direction, disengagement, squad retreat status, stagger recovery and terminal death.
